ZERO APP DEPENDENCIES
01

A physical foundation

CC0 card and casino-chip recordings, edited into new gestures. Real, irregular transients replace generic synth clicks. Frequent actions have three alternate takes.

02

A designed harmonic layer

Original damped resonances, soft harmonic blooms, moving filtered air and windowed foley grains. Low impacts carry midrange harmonics so they are not all inaudible sub-bass.

03

Space, restraint, hierarchy

Diffuse stereo rooms, gentle transient control, quiet overlays and category-based levels. Full-quality 48 kHz / 16-bit stereo SFX previews, with headroom and click-free edges. Original music uses compact AAC loops, just as it does in the game.

Studio is selected for the game; the alternatives remain available for comparison. No measurement is a substitute for your listening judgment. Review the mix against animations and real iPhone speakers.

Production options & commercial-use licensingSources, tradeoffs, and what is actually included

Included: original compositions and synthesis, CC0 foley, and standard-library-only offline renderers with offline AAC encoding. The app has no new library or service dependency. Source licenses and hashes travel with this soundboard.

Software and audio have different licenses. An open-source editor does not automatically make an imported sample commercially usable; a GPL production tool does not automatically make its original audio output GPL.
