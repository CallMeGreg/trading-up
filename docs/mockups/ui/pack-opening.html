Clean cut is the selected direction. Drag across the very top of its pack, then release.

01 / ASelected

Clean cut

Light across the seam. One crisp release.

Keyboard / assistive action · always available while sealed

Sealed. Slice the fine line across the top.

The cue
A hairline light sweeps along the top seal. “Swipe to open” is the only visible instruction, including while dragging or using either opening preference.
The finish · 0.64s
A bright cut follows your finger. The top strip snaps free and sails away as the wrapper drops.
Selected direction
Clean, quick, and focused on the pack. Extra guidance stays available to assistive controls, not on the opening screen.
02 / BMore tactile

Foil ribbon

Feel the seal give way under your finger.

Keyboard / assistive action · always available while sealed

Sealed. Start at either end of the foil seam.

The cue
Tiny notches in the seal, not added handles. The metallic edge does most of the teaching.
The finish · 0.78s
The strip bends up as you slice, flashes its silver underside, then curls away on release. Reverse or cancel and it settles back.
The trade-off
More physical feedback and less glowing UI, but a slightly longer finish than Clean cut.
03 / CMore cinematic

Cut + lift

A little more anticipation before the first card.

Keyboard / assistive action · always available while sealed

Sealed. Slice, release, and watch the card stack rise.

The cue
The same restrained cut line, with a softer shimmer and an almost-white backdrop. Nothing competes with the pack.
The finish · 1.10s
The cap floats off, the sleeve falls, and a stack of card backs lifts into view before the first reveal.
The trade-off
The most ceremonial option. Auto open still bypasses the whole sequence when you want speed.
